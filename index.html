Kept is an iPhone and iPad app that works out your UK take-home pay for the 2026/27 tax year (and 2025/26 for comparison), then explains it in plain English.

What it covers

  • Income tax for England, Wales & Northern Ireland, and all six Scottish bands
  • The Personal Allowance taper above £100,000 (the "60% trap")
  • Class 1, Class 2 and Class 4 National Insurance
  • Student loan Plans 1, 2, 4 and 5 and the Postgraduate Loan
  • Salary sacrifice, net pay and relief-at-source pensions
  • Dividends, savings interest and the High Income Child Benefit Charge

Every rate is listed in the app with a link to its GOV.UK source. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Kept gives estimates for general information only. It is not financial, tax or legal advice and is not affiliated with HMRC or the UK Government.
