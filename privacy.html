Privacy policy

Effective 18 October 2026

This policy covers the Kept app for iPhone and iPad ("the app"), published by Joshua Harty.

Summary

  • The app does not collect any personal data.
  • All calculations happen on your device.
  • There are no accounts, adverts, analytics or tracking.

Information you enter

Figures you type, such as salary, pension contributions or the number of children, are used only to calculate results on your device. Your most recent inputs are saved in the app's local storage on your device so they are there when you reopen it. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete them at any time with "Clear my saved inputs" on the Rates screen, or by deleting the app.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software and makes no network requests of its own. Links to GOV.UK and this website open in your web browser, where those sites' own policies apply.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app's data practices ever change, this page and the App Store privacy details will be updated before the change is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised on the support page.

Kept gives estimates for general information only. It is not financial, tax or legal advice and is not affiliated with HMRC or the UK Government.
