Common questions

Why is my payslip different?
Kept works on annual figures with a standard tax code (1257L). Payroll calculates tax and National Insurance per pay period, and your tax code may include adjustments for benefits, underpaid tax or allowances. Bonuses and irregular pay can also change per-period National Insurance.

Which tax year does it use?
2026/27 (6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027) by default. You can switch to 2025/26 at the top of the screen. Rates are updated in a free app update each April.

Which student loan plan am I on?
Your payslip or your Student Loans Company online account shows it. Plan 4 is for Scottish loans; Plan 5 is for courses starting from August 2023 in England.

How do I enter a relief-at-source pension?
Enter the gross contribution, including the 20% the provider claims from HMRC. Kept shows what it costs you and gives higher-rate relief by extending your basic rate band.

Does Kept store my information?
Only on your device, so your last inputs are there next time. You can clear them from the Rates screen. See the privacy policy.
